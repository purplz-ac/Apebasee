Ape Base v6: starting...

Ape Base: grab gold bars (3 at a time), carry them to the teal recycler pad, bank 8 to escape. Don't get caught by the monster.

Quest: tap "VR" (bottom right). Push off the floor, walls and crates with your fists to run, leap and climb. Backup: left stick walks, right stick turns, A jumps. The monster can't reach you above 1.2 m.

Desktop test: WASD move, drag mouse to look, Space jump, R restart.
